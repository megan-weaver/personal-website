About Me

Hello! I'm Megan, a student at Villanova University studying Mathematics and French with a minor in Computer Science. My projects vary from modeling public health trends to analyzing cryptographic systems that govern our digital world. In short, I'm inspired by my lived experiences and those of others, and seek to find meaning through math. I'm particularly interested in the fields of climate and geographic sciences, and how the changing climate impacts infectious disease spread and population displacement.
